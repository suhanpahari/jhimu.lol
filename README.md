# Jhimu's Island 3D

A textured first-person voxel island you explore as **Jhimu** — walk it, dig it, build on it, and play four mini-games scattered across the shore. Minecraft-ish in feel, inspired by Google's *Champion Island* doodle.

Play it: **https://jhimu.lol**

## What's in it

- A first-person **voxel island** (160×48×160) built procedurally — beaches, forests, a maple village, a mirror lake, mountains with snow caps.
- **Dig & build**: break blocks and place them back; wood, planks, stone, sand, flowers and more.
- **Caves & ore underground**: carved cave systems with coal, iron, gold and crystal to find.
- **Mobs**: sheep, chickens and hopping slimes wander the island.
- **Four mini-games**, each started by walking onto a glowing flower pad (press **E** or tap **Play**):
  - **Cove Parkour** — hop the floating planks over the sea to the crystal goal. Timed.
  - **Block Smash** — smash the lit-up block as many times as you can in 30s.
  - **Critter Round-Up** — tag 6 penned critters before time runs out.
  - **Relic Hunt** — follow the hot / cold banner to dig up four buried relics.
- **Day & night on the real clock in India Standard Time** — bright at midday IST, dark at midnight IST.
- Everything is drawn procedurally — **no image or audio assets**, one single file, rendered with Three.js.

## Controls

- **Move**: WASD / arrow keys, or the on-screen joystick on phones.
- **Look**: mouse (click to lock the pointer), or drag on phones.
- **Jump**: Space, or the jump button on phones.
- **Break / place**: left-click / right-click, or the on-screen **BREAK** / **PLACE** buttons.
- **Start a game / interact**: **E**, or the on-screen button when you're on a pad.

Built to be played mostly on a phone.
