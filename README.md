# Jhimu's Island

A cozy, large handcrafted pixel island you explore as **Jhimu** — a little girl who's just stepped off the ferry. Inspired by the feel of Google's *Champion Island* doodle.

Play it: **https://jhimu.lol**

## What's in it

- A big island (220×220 tiles) with **14 named regions** to discover — beaches, forests, mountains, a village, old ruins, a lighthouse, a swamp, a hidden grove, and more.
- **Exploration-first**: a minimap, a discovery system, signposts and dirt paths connecting the landmarks.
- **Gather & make**: chop wood, mine stone, pick berries, herbs, mushrooms and crystals; craft torches, planks and a fishing net.
- **Survival touches**: health and energy, campfires and wells that restore you, eat berries to heal.
- **Day & night that follows the real clock in India Standard Time** — the island is bright at midday IST and dark at midnight IST; a torch lights your way at night.
- Five islanders to talk to, each with their own dialogue.
- Everything is drawn procedurally on an HTML canvas — **no image or audio assets**, one single file.
- Progress is saved in your browser automatically.

## Controls

- **Move**: Arrow keys / WASD, or the on-screen D-pad on phones.
- **Interact / gather / talk**: Space, Enter, E — or the **A** button on phones.
- **Pack / Make / Journal**: I or Tab — or the **☰** button on phones.

Built to be played mostly on a phone.
